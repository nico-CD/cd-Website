---

## Visibility recommendation (face vs logo)

**Do a short face intro (5-8 seconds), then go screen-only.**

Shop owners buy from a person, not a logo. Keep it casual: phone or webcam, plain background, no studio theatrics. After the intro, stay on the product so the proof is the engine, not a talking head.

Skip a full talking-head VSL. Mom-and-pop owners care that the system works on a phone at 9pm.

---

## Prep checklist

1. Open in order: `demo.carrillodynamics.com` → `hvac.carrillodynamics.com`
2. Browser zoom ~110-125%. Hide bookmarks. Quiet room. Hard-refresh both.
3. HVAC: EN on. Know the path: **Request Service → Emergency → Submit → Tech Portal → Requests**
4. Hub: know where **Ver en Español** / EN|ES sits for the bilingual beat
5. End card ready full-screen (Canva / slides / Notion):

```
CARRILLO DYNAMICS
Book a 15-min strategy call
carrillodynamics.com/start
```

6. Do **not** click on camera: electrical `/app` or `/dispatch`, waste `/app`, roofing `/command`, empty towing/landscaping boards, "Demo PoC" footers

## Tone rules

- Mom-and-pop / family shop energy, not enterprise SaaS
- Local service businesses anywhere (don't lean hard into Chicago geography)
- Bilingual is a feature beat, not a footnote
- No pricing, packages, or retainers
- No em dashes in spoken lines or on-screen end card copy
- Value = capture leads, cut chaos, free capacity for the crew you already have

---

## After record

1. Export 1080p. Burn or upload captions (owners often watch muted).
2. Upload **YouTube Unlisted**. Copy the watch or embed URL.
3. Drop the embed into `/start` (replace the coming-soon block). Keep Watch → Book → optional demos path.
4. Hard-refresh `/start` on a phone before handing out cards.
5. When you reprint cards later, `/start` can greet first-edition scanners with a short "you've got the first card run" note if the URL stays the same.
